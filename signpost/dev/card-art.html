Signposts: card art

The signpost renderer's card pictures, as chosen Nov 6 (icon D and banner 4b: claude/signpost-renderer-icon-sheet.html in the Foundry project): a trail signpost in the Places colours, a dotted trail at its foot; the banner laid out like the Places banner, on a faint map, the boards saying CHICAGO and SANTA MONICA. Open this file in a browser (it needs no server), check the two pictures, then save each into signpost\tile\ under the name on its button. The banner's words use your system's fonts.
